The English in this patch follows the Japanese closely, preserving original names (possible for ALMOST ALL proper nouns by using a variable width font), terminology, and honorifics. Text was translated 100% using AI; however, dialogue was translated, passed over again checking for inconsistencies, and any changes were proposed, checked with various translation tools by myself, and then either accepted, rejected, or tweaked slightly before accepting. A small number of proper nouns and lines were forced to be condensed, as there was no space for them.

After completing the game, New Game + is unlocked with both regular and hard mode, where enemies scale to match your level while also giving more variety in Medarots fought.

New Game + carries over
-----------------------
-Medals, levels, names, and Medaforce
-Parts and Medarot builds
-Lifetime Wins/Battles
-Clear count

Story Medals are held back and returned at appropriate points while retaining their information from initial run. Enemy teams scale to the player's progression, with levels capped at 100. Generic opponents can receive different Medarots or mixed parts, while story characters retain their original teams.

New Game+ Also gives the opportunity to collect missing parts. From the first New Game+ clear onward, players receive 10 missing part sets and the Black Kaburo Medal. Defeating certain characters can award their medals, including the other 9 missing Medals normally requiring link trades.

Additional Fixes

-Settings menu has a new data erase option
-Faster enemy AI
-Wins/Battles display up to 999
-Variable-width font for Medarots, Medals, and Parts
